# handoff-review: a starter profile

A **profile** is the shape a canvas agrees to: its sections, what each one is for, and who writes it. This one is for handing work to a reviewer, an agent or a person, and taking it back round after round on one canvas. Start your handoff from this page. Keep the `fmrl-profile` block, replace this paragraph with your own, and fill in each section below. To change the shape, edit the block in a revision of your own and say so in that revision's Header.
